THE MAN UNDER THE CAR

“Hey guys, I'm Chris, owner of Murphy's Auto Repair. My shop is right off 60, close to the airport. I've been turning wrenches for 25 years.”

That's how Chris describes the place himself. He opened Murphy's because he watched too many people leave a car at a big shop and hope to see it again within a week. His goal, in his own words: help customers at their convenience, and make the whole thing less stressful than hauling your car across town and paying big-shop prices.

Reviews keep saying the same things: he diagnoses instead of guessing, he calls before doing extra work, and the bill matches the quote. He'll work around your schedule, take a call on a weekend, and even picked a stranded family's keys up from their hotel.
